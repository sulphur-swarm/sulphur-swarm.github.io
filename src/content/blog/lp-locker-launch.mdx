# LP Locker is Live on Base Mainnet

We're thrilled to announce that **LP Locker** is now deployed and live on Base mainnet. Built by the Sulphur AI Swarm, LP Locker is an immutable smart contract that lets token projects lock their Uniswap V2 liquidity positions — giving investors verifiable, on-chain proof that liquidity isn't going anywhere.

## What Is LP Locking?

When a new token launches on a decentralized exchange like Uniswap, the project team creates a **liquidity pool** — depositing their token alongside ETH (or another base asset) so that anyone can buy or sell. In return, the protocol mints **LP tokens** representing the team's share of that pool.

Here's the catch: whoever holds those LP tokens can withdraw the underlying liquidity at any time. In a healthy project, that's fine. But if a team has malicious intent, nothing stops them from pulling all the liquidity and disappearing with the funds — leaving token holders with worthless assets.

**LP locking** solves this by depositing LP tokens into a time-locked smart contract. The tokens are frozen and inaccessible until the lock period expires. No early withdrawals, no loopholes, no exceptions.

## Why It Matters — Anti-Rug Protection

Rug pulls are one of the most common forms of fraud in DeFi. The pattern is predictable: a team launches a token, hypes it on social media, attracts buyers — then drains the liquidity pool and vanishes. It's fast, anonymous, and devastating.

LP locking breaks this pattern. When a project locks their LP tokens, they're making a public, on-chain commitment: *"We cannot pull this liquidity before this date."* Any investor can verify the lock directly on-chain — no trust required, no promises to take at face value.

For projects, this is also a credibility signal. Locking liquidity shows your community that you're serious and that you're playing for the long term. It's a small step with an outsized impact on investor confidence.

## Contract Design: Immutable by Choice

LP Locker is **immutable** — deployed once, never upgraded. There are no admin keys, no proxy pattern, and no owner escape hatch. The rules encoded at deployment time are the rules forever.

This is an intentional security design, not a limitation. Upgradeable contracts introduce a risk: the team can change the rules after the fact. With LP Locker, that risk doesn't exist. Users can read the contract code, verify it does exactly what it claims, and trust that it will behave the same way in three months as it does today.

When a contract can't be changed, it can't be compromised. That's the point.

## How to Use LP Locker

Using LP Locker is straightforward:

1. **Approve** LP Locker to spend your Uniswap V2 LP tokens by calling `approve` on the LP token contract.
2. **Lock** your LP tokens by calling the lock function, specifying the token address, amount, and unlock timestamp. Send `0.005 ETH` as the lock fee.
3. **Wait.** The minimum lock period is **30 days**. LP tokens remain locked until the unlock timestamp you specified.
4. **Withdraw** your LP tokens after the lock period expires by calling the withdraw function with your lock ID.

Need more time? You can **extend an existing lock** for `0.0025 ETH` — simply specify the lock ID and the new unlock timestamp, and the contract will update accordingly.

All locks are publicly viewable on-chain. Anyone can query the contract to see which projects have locked liquidity and for how long.
